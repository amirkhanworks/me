01 / IoT wearables · GCC · India

CamelX and WagVitals

A field wearable built to collect live biometric data from racing camels in the desert, plus a dog activity tracker now at prototype stage.

Problem

A GCC client needed live biometric data from racing camels in the field. ECG, SpO2, gait, speed and temperature, sent over cellular from a device that survives the desert.

What I did

Led the commercial side as co-founder: client relationship, pilot scope, commercial terms and the path from pilot to volume. Our engineering team built the hardware on ESP32-S3 with a 4-layer PCB, LTE and GNSS.

What changed

The device went from build to a field pilot and into volume negotiations. The client is anonymised for confidentiality.

02 / Hardware launch · India

Luminox Smart Home

Retrofit automation designed around the existing switchboard, not a renovation project.

Problem

Indian homes want automation but not renovation. Most systems need rewiring, multiple site visits, and a buyer who knows exactly what they want.

What I did

Shaped positioning, pricing model and launch plan for a retrofit system that installs behind the existing switchboard in one visit. Ran field research and a full competitor teardown of the Indian market.

What changed

A patent-pending product with a clear story for homeowners, renters and families with elderly parents, plus a waitlist built before launch.

How I work

The operating principles behind the work.

01

Start from the buyer.

Every technical decision is checked against one question: does this make the product easier to understand, buy or install?

02

Small teams, AI-first.

Research, positioning, decks and sites built in days, not weeks. AI is part of the workflow, so a small team covers the ground of a large one.

03

Only verified numbers.

No rounded-up claims. If a figure isn't confirmed, it isn't published, here or in a pitch.
