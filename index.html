Luminox Engineering: CamelX and WagVitals

IoT wearables · GCC · India

Problem

A GCC client needed live biometric data from racing camels in the field. ECG, SpO2, gait, speed and temperature, sent over cellular from a device that survives the desert.

What I did

Led the commercial side as co-founder: client relationship, pilot scope, commercial terms and the path from pilot to volume. Our engineering team built the hardware on ESP32-S3 with a 4-layer PCB, LTE and GNSS.

What changed

The device went from build to a field pilot and into volume negotiations. The client is anonymised for confidentiality.

Luminox Smart Home

Essay · Pricing

Build the sales process around how people decide

Our first instinct on pricing was an engineer's instinct: ask the customer how many lights, fans and switchboards they have.

That's the right question for a quote. It's the wrong question for a buyer.

Most people don't know. They'd have to walk around the house counting. Most won't bother.
