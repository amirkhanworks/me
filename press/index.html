Long bio

Amir Khan is co-founder of Luminox Automation in Mumbai. The company has a patent-pending retrofit smart-home product and an engineering services business. His work has also included wearable telemetry, including a biometric wearable piloted in the GCC, as well as enterprise cloud delivery and AI SaaS sales. At IFI Techsolutions, he managed a team of 16 engineers working across 15 enterprise clients and 10+ Azure migrations. The team worked with enterprise clients including Larsen & Toubro and Mahindra & Mahindra. In London, he built a £2M+ enterprise pipeline at FutureMatch/Recruitics, including Aston Martin, JCB and Check Point. At Breakbottle, he worked in growth and closed deals with Sage Capital and Imperial Business School. Earlier, Amir took Luminox's IoT smart-home product from concept to launch. He holds an MSc from Birkbeck, University of London, and the PMP and PMI-CPMAI credentials. He co-authored a paper on digital transformation and venture capital in startup ecosystems. Luminox was shortlisted for TEJAS at IIM Mumbai in 2026.
