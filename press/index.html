Long bio · 150 words

Amir Khan builds technical products and takes them to market. He is co-founder of Luminox Automation, a Mumbai IoT company with a patent-pending retrofit smart home system and an engineering services arm whose work includes a biometric wearable piloted in the GCC. Luminox has been shortlisted for TEJAS at IIM Mumbai and was part of ISB's ISLAB Pre-Accelerator Program. Before Luminox, Amir managed a 16-person engineering team at IFI Techsolutions, a leading Microsoft partner, delivering 10+ Azure migrations for enterprise clients including Larsen & Toubro and Mahindra & Mahindra. In the UK he built a £2M+ enterprise pipeline at FutureMatch/Recruitics and closed institutional deals at Breakbottle. He holds an MSc from Birkbeck, University of London, is PMP and PMI-CPMAI certified, and co-authored a paper on digital transformation and venture capital in startup ecosystems.
